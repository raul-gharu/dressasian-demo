Dress Asian - mobile app concept demo (by Raul Gharu)

Open index.html in any browser (double-click works). On a laptop it shows inside a phone frame;
on a phone it fills the screen. Everything runs offline - images and fonts are in this folder.

Listings, prices, seller first names, sizes and photos come from public listings on
dressasian.co.uk (captured 9 Nov 2026). Conversations, the profile, wallet, alerts and the
AI listing assistant are simulated for demonstration only.

Shortcuts: index.html?s=home | browse | detail | offer | sell | scan | ai | done | alerts | inbox | chat | profile
